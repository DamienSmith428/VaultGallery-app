Privacy
Policy.

Last updated: 6/8/2026

🔒

The short version: we collect nothing.

Our apps do not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data — ever. There are no accounts, no analytics, no crash reporting, and no telemetry of any kind. Everything you do stays on your device.

Information We Collect

We collect no information whatsoever. Our apps have no backend servers, no databases, and no mechanism to receive data from your device. No personal information, usage statistics, device identifiers, or behavioral data of any kind is gathered.

Data Storage

Any data created while using our apps — such as preferences, saved content, or files — is stored locally on your device only. We have no access to this data, and it is never transmitted anywhere outside of your device.

Network Activity

Our apps only make network requests when directly necessary for the features you choose to use. No data is routed through servers owned or operated by us. Any network activity goes directly between your device and the relevant third-party service you interact with.

Third-Party Services

Our apps may use open-source libraries or interact with third-party platforms as part of their core functionality. Any such libraries run entirely on your device and do not independently send your data anywhere. Any interaction with third-party platforms is initiated solely by you and governed by those platforms' own privacy policies.

Permissions

Our apps request only the permissions necessary to deliver the features they offer. No sensitive permissions — such as access to contacts, location, microphone, or camera — are requested unless they are directly required for a feature. Permissions are never used for data collection or tracking purposes.

Children's Privacy

Since our apps collect no data from anyone, there is no additional risk or consideration for users of any age, including children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy is ever updated, the revised version will be posted here with a new effective date. Because we collect no data, significant changes are unlikely. We encourage you to check back periodically.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, feel free to open an issue on GitHub.
